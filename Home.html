About Us

Established in 2010, The Tattoo Co. specializes in fine-line and minimalist tattoo designs, in black and white or in color. We're in the heart of Boston, easy to reach by subway, bus and commuter rail, and surrounded by restaurants and cafes.

We've been a customer favorite for years, especially because we were one of the first shops to focus only on minimalist designs. We use safe, sterilized tools, and keeping you clean and comfortable is our top priority.
