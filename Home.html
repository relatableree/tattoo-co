About Us

Established in 2010, we are The Tattoo Co. We specialize in line and minimalist tattoo designs, in both black and white and color. We are located in the heart of Boston, easy to reach by subway, bus and commuter rail, among plenty of restaurants and cafes.

We have been a customer favorite for a long time, especially because we were one of the first shops to offer minimalist designs exclusively. We use very safe tools, and customer sanitation is our top priority.
